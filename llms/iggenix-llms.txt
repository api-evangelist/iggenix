# IgGenix

> IgGenix, Inc. is a clinical-stage antibody discovery and development company in South San Francisco,
> California, founded in 2019 on work from Stephen Quake's laboratory at Stanford with allergy
> clinician-researcher Kari Nadeau. Its SEQ SIFTER platform uses single-cell RNA sequencing to capture
> the rare IgE-producing B cells in allergic patients' blood and re-engineers them into fully human,
> allergen-specific IgG antibodies that block the allergic cascade. Lead program IGNX001 is a human IgG4
> monoclonal antibody for peanut allergy, in the Phase 1 ACCELERATE Peanut trial.

## How to use it

- Base URL: `https://iggenix.com/wp-json` — no credential needed for reads
- Start at `GET /wp/v2/search?search=peanut`, then branch on `subtype` and follow `_links.self`
- Paging: `page`, `per_page` (max 100), `offset`; counts in `X-WP-Total` / `X-WP-TotalPages`
- Trim payloads with `_fields`; resolve relations in one call with `_embed`
- Errors are the WordPress envelope, not RFC 9457: `{"code":"...","message":"...","data":{"status":404}}` — branch on `code`
- No rate limits published, no `X-RateLimit-*` headers; `robots.txt` asks for `Crawl-delay: 10`
